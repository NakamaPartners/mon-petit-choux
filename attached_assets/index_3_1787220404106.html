chou

French · noun

  1. Cabbage.
  2. Pâte à choux: the piped dough that puffs into a cream puff or an eclair shell.
  3. What a French grandmother calls a child she adores.

The name is a small joke that happens to also be true.

We are known for our pâte à choux, which we use in several of our most requested products. Customers who have lived all over the world have told us our eclairs and cream puffs are the best they have ever had.

Wedding and celebration cakes still run through our sister atelier, Maribelle Cakery.

About the shoppe

Three things worth knowing.

01Early Mornings

Our bakers start work before the sun rises each day to ensure that every batch is as fresh as possible before it gets to you. The warm smell of baking often escapes onto the street before we have even opened. We are dedicated to kickstarting your morning with warm croissants, buttery danishes and a cup of hot tea. Our bakery also has desserts and delicious cakes, and you can even get cake by the slice.

02Handcrafted Goods

We work with local suppliers to source the freshest and most authentic ingredients, and use old-fashioned techniques to ensure the highest quality. Our goal is to make your tastebuds happy.

03A Cozy Place to Enjoy

Located in Cheviot, we have a nice indoor and outdoor space to enjoy your pastries.
